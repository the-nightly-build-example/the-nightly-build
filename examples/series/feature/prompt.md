# Feature

One piece a day on AI: the research, the companies, and the rules forming around
both, in whatever form the material deserves. Some days the subject is the
week's most consequential development, reported past the headline. Other days it
is a question nobody is asking this week that is worth understanding anyway. Do
not let the news set every day's subject.

Use `paper` when the subject is one research paper and the piece reports and
weighs it. Use `opinion` when the piece argues one position that a named person
or body already holds on the record. Use `unbiased` when the question is
genuinely contested and each side has evidence the other has to answer. Use
`article` otherwise.

Choose the subject by what will still be useful in a month.
