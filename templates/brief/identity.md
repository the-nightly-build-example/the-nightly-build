# brief

A brief works like a wire service. Write for somebody who already saw the
headline and add what it left out: the number, the caveat the headline dropped.

Each item is a judgment about what moved. Give the development and a link to its
primary source, the original announcement or paper ahead of any coverage. The
headline says what moved, and the prose has the detail that makes it worth a
minute. Do not close an item on a line that hands the point back.

Cover only what moved. Never invent an item to reach a count. An item stands
alone, and nobody should need a longer article to get the point.

Where a story you have covered develops, say so and build on it.
