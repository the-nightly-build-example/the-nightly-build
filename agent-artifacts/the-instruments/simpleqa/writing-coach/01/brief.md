## What this piece is
A lesson that teaches how one measurement is constructed and how to read it: the
SimpleQA factuality benchmark. Domain: measurement and evaluation of AI systems.
Genre: explanatory writing that takes apart a number people quote — where it
comes from, what it supports, and how it misleads.

## What the voice guide is for
Study writers who are good at teaching a reader to distrust a number without
cynicism: who explain how a statistic is made and what it can and cannot bear,
with concrete cases. The best exemplars here handle evaluation, statistics, or
measurement plainly and hold claims to what the evidence supports. Find them
among writers the field rates for clear quantitative explanation.

An exemplar that itself discusses SimpleQA or LLM factuality benchmarks needs
care: quote it only where the passage shows the writing and not the findings this
article will report, or pick a different piece.
