# researcher brief: the-instruments/toxicity-score (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- ../../commission.md — the angle, boundaries, source-count policy

Output: evidence.md (in this directory)

This round's focus: pin, against the RealToxicityPrompts paper, the prompt count
and the exact definitions of its two headline metrics (expected maximum toxicity
and probability of a toxic continuation). Establish, against the primary that owns
it, how the Perspective API classifier is built (its training data — Wikipedia
Talk / Civil Comments — and that its output is a probability, not a verdict) and
what its own documentation concedes. For the misleading case, read firsthand the
primaries that own the bias findings (e.g. Sap et al. 2019 on racial bias in
hate-speech detection; and any Jigsaw/Perspective evaluation of identity-term
bias) and record the exact quantified findings. Search for contradictions,
including defenses of the metric. Classify sources primary/secondary by authorship
and stake; resolve every URL to the document's own page. Meet the policy: >=8
sources, >=4 primary, >=1 secondary.
