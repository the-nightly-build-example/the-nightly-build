# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/rouge (01)

Inputs:

- `commission.md` (at the artifact root) — the measurement, angle, neighbors, and
  habits not to inherit.
- `editorial-direction.md` (at the artifact root) — house standard, slop, headline
  standard, press voice, lesson identity, and the series prompt.

Output: `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md` (under this article's artifact root).

This round: the genre is a plain-language teardown of how one evaluation number is
produced, for a smart reader new to it. Study writers who explain a statistic or a
scoring method honestly, make a mechanical measure (string overlap against a
reference) intuitive with a small worked example, and stay even-handed about a
metric that is neither perfect nor worthless. Prefer explainers who can carry a
small numerical argument for a general audience over writers who only editorialize
about benchmarks.

Break the paper-wide opener formula ("By the end you will know X. You will also
see Y") and do not model The Instruments' recent "every flagship ships an X score"
opener.

Run environment: outbound web is available through a proxy. Retrieve the raw text
of every exemplar page and verify each quotation word for word against it before
reporting, per your skill.
