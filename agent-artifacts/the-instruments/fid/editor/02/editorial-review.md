# Editorial review: the-instruments/fid (editor/02)

Focused confirmation read, scoped by the editor/02 brief to the single routed
fix from editor/01 (the Why-card Formula closer) and to whether the rewrite
introduced anything new. Settled objections are not reopened.

## Skeptic

The only new prose is the Why card's closing sentence. It carries no new claim:
it names the calculation the lesson builds (image to score) and the three inputs
the body already documents and cites — sample count (s5, s6), feature network
(s4), resizing (s7). No unsupported assertion enters. The old closer's evaluative
promise ("which FID comparisons carry weight and which ones only look like they
do") is gone, and with it the one place the closer graded comparisons rather than
described the reader's payoff, so nothing new needs sourcing. Every figure, the
annotated Fréchet equation, the eight data-nb-kind labels, and the chart are
untouched from the editor/01-approved state.

## Cut

Slop pass over the rewritten sentence and its paragraph. The new closer —
"Work through both passes and you can follow FID's calculation yourself, from an
image to a score, and read a reported FID gap knowing whether the sample count,
the feature network, or the resizing had room to move it" — does not open with
"By the end you [will/can]…" and does not follow the shelf's "take any … claim
and [say what it does / does not]" shape. It is off the cross-article Formula
that editor/01 named. Construction is now lesson-specific: an imperative
("Work through both passes") into a concrete two-part payoff, unlike the eleven
sibling Why cards that share the "By the end…" cadence.

The sentence is not a signpost. It states the reader's earned capability, which
is the lesson Why card's required work, and it does so in FID's own particulars
rather than restating the article's method. No banned em-dash or semicolon
reflex; the one internal comma series is a plain list. "Hold onto that." stays
cut. No new slop, no borrowed exemplar clause, no prompt leakage — "both passes"
describes the article's build-then-turn structure, not a planning label.

## Reader

Reading the Why card straight through, the closer now tells the declared reader
what the two passes leave them holding — the calculation end to end, and the
three levers to check behind a reported gap — without the prior sentence's
verdict framing. It reads as the article's own voice, not a house template. The
rest of the piece is the editor/01-approved article, so its reader answer stands.

## Edits

- None. Confirmation read only; no direct change made.

## Required work

- None.

## Decision

approve — the Why-card closer is rewritten off the shelf-wide Formula into FID's
own particulars, still states the reader's end-state as the lesson identity
requires, and introduces no new claim, grammatical break, or slop; the rest is
unchanged from the editor/01-approved state.
