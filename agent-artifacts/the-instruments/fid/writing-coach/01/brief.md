# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/fid (01)

Inputs:
- `../../commission.md` — the assignment: anatomize the number behind "best image
  generator" (FID) and show a documented way it misleads.
- `../../editorial-direction.md` — house standard, the paper's voice, series prompt.

Output: `voice-guide.md` (this directory)

This round's focus: the piece explains a statistical measurement procedure —
network features, Gaussians, a distance — and has to make it concrete and gripping
for a smart non-specialist, then turn skeptical about the number without dismissing
it. Study writers who make a measurement's construction vivid, who can walk a
reader through "here is exactly what this metric computes" and then "here is how it
fools people," and who convey doubt about a statistic precisely rather than
cynically. Prefer writers on measurement, statistics, or evaluation. Choose
exemplars on other subjects so their material cannot leak into this piece.
