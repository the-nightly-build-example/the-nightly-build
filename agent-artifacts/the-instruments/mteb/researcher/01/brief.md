# researcher brief: the-instruments/mteb (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- ../../commission.md — the measurement, the angle to test, the exact facts to verify, and the source policy

Output: evidence.md (this directory)

The commission's "angle to test" names the mechanism to source (per-dataset
metric → task-type grouping → plain average → leaderboard rank) and the facts to
pin against their primary: the task-type and dataset counts for a dated MTEB
version, the per-task metrics, and the exact contamination/imbalance concern the
maintainers acknowledged and what their revision changed. Keep the benchmark and
the leaderboard distinct.
