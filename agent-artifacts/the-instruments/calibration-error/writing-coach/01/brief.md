This lesson explains how a reliability number is computed and where it misleads.
Study writers who make a statistic concrete for an intelligent general reader,
walk a small calculation without notation walls, and hold a metric to what it can
actually certify. Value clear quantitative explanation and skeptical calibration
about summary numbers. Choose passages that show a measurement being built up
step by step and then questioned, not passages that carry claims about
calibration, ECE, or model confidence themselves.
