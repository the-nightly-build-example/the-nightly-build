  RewardBench 2 section. Press editorial (`press/editorial.md`) forbids this:
  "The takeaway bookend is where a lesson lands its judgment. Do not close the
  body with a Verdict note, or any block that restates the finding. Some older
  articles still carry that block from the paper's earlier template. It is a
  leftover, not a model to copy." Cut the Verdict note (or the equivalent
  restating block, whatever name it carries in the draft). Fold anything real
  it carries into the surrounding prose or the takeaway itself. This is not
  optional.
