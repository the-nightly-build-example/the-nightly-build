# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/superglue (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — house standard, paper
  voice, series prompt, lesson template identity
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the measurement, angle, reader, boundaries

Output: voice-guide.md (this directory)

The article is a lesson explaining how one benchmark number (the SuperGLUE score)
is built and why "model beats human baseline" misled people. The reader is smart
and non-technical. Study writers who make measurement and statistics vivid and
skeptical without jargon or hype — the register the paper voice describes. The
craft problem is taking a leaderboard number apart into the choices behind it
(an average across mixed metrics, a constructed human baseline) and keeping the
reader oriented the whole way.
