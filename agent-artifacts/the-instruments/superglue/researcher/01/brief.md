# researcher brief: the-instruments/superglue (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — citation standard,
  series territory, declared reader
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the measurement, angle, source floor

Output: evidence.md (this directory)

Build the evidence record for how the SuperGLUE number is produced and how it
misled. From the primary sources:

- The eight SuperGLUE tasks, each task's metric, and exactly how the leaderboard
  aggregates them into one score (the averaging/weighting rule). Quote the paper
  on the aggregation.
- How the human baseline was constructed (annotator protocol, per-task human
  scores, the aggregate human number). Get the exact human-baseline figure and
  its method from the paper.
- The exact model and date that first exceeded the SuperGLUE human baseline, from
  a primary (leaderboard or model paper), and the reported scores on both sides.
  Confirm this against the leaderboard, not just coverage.
- Documented task artifacts / shortcut features and the benchmark's saturation
  (later commentary or the authors' own notes).
- At least one secondary source showing how the human-baseline crossing was
  reported to the public ("AI beats humans...").

Source floor: at least 8 sources, at least 4 primary, at least 1 secondary.
Classify each and say why. Record in Numbers every score the argument leans on
with its owner and scope. Note the GLUE→SuperGLUE saturation link (GLUE paper
1804.07461) so the writer can reference the taught GLUE lesson. Confirm every URL
resolves to the source's own page; record the leaderboard URL as it lives.
