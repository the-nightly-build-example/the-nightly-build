# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/glue (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, lesson identity, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the exact subject, angle, and required contribution

Output: ./voice-guide.md

Focus: the genre is a lesson that opens up a headline number the public reads as
"AI reached human level" and shows how it is manufactured (an average over tasks,
against a thin human baseline) and where it breaks (shortcuts, artifacts). Study
writers who explain a measurement or a benchmark plainly and puncture a metric
without smugness. Calibrate craft for prose that stays exact about procedure and
figures while keeping a skeptical, non-cynical stance a lay reader can follow.
