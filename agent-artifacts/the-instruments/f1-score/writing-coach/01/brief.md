The article explains a foundational evaluation metric and its failure modes for a
smart reader new to it. Study writers who make statistics and measurement legible
to a serious general audience without dumbing the math down (the kind of writer
the field rates for readable quantitative reasoning). The craft problem is teaching
precision, recall, and a harmonic mean through holdable counts, and holding a
skeptical-but-fair stance toward a number everyone reports. Do not let an exemplar
hand the writer this article's own findings about F1.
