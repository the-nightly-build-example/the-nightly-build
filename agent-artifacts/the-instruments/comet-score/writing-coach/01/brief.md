This is an explanatory lesson that takes apart a machine-learning evaluation
metric for a smart general reader who does not work with translation systems. The
craft challenge is making a statistical measurement concrete and showing how a
number can be trustworthy and misleading at once, without lecturing. Study
writers who explain measurement, statistics, or evaluation to non-specialists and
stay skeptical without posturing. Show the writing, not this article's own
findings about COMET or BLEU.
