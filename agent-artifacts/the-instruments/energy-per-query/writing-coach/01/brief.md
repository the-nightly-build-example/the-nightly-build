## Domain and genre
Numerate explanatory journalism about energy, computing, and measurement claims
for a smart general reader. Genre: the piece that dismantles a scary,
widely-shared number by showing how it was built and where it bends. Study
writers the field rates for exactly this move: careful data explainers who
handle units, denominators, and uncertainty honestly (for example the tradition
of energy/computing analysts and numerate explainer writers who show their
arithmetic and refuse false precision). Pick your own ≥3 exemplars; prefer the
primary piece; skip SEO/influencer content; do not treat any provider blog as a
voice exemplar.

## What the voice guide must solve for this article
- Making numbers legible: how to give each figure a comparison the reader
  already holds (a watt-hour as seconds of a household appliance), and how to
  present a 10x spread between two honest estimates without hedging into mush.
- Handling uncertainty with a committed voice: saying plainly what is measured,
  what is estimated, and what is extrapolated, without "it depends" throat-
  clearing.
- Correcting a viral claim cleanly through fact, not through a "not X but Y"
  reflex.
