# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/bertscore (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, and boundaries for this lesson
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop and headline standards, the paper's voice, the lesson template identity, and the series prompt

Output: voice-guide.md (in this directory)

Domain and genre: an explanatory lesson that opens up one evaluation metric, how
it is computed and where it misleads, for a smart reader with no time in a
codebase. Study writers who make a measurement's machinery vivid and are precise
about what a number does and does not license. Quote passages that show how to
walk through a calculation in plain words and how to state a limitation without
hedging; skip passages whose worth is their own subject.

This round's focus: the piece explains a computation (embed, match, average) and
must keep the reader oriented while being blunt that a BERTScore number is not
comparable across setups. Show voices that hold a method and its caveat together.
