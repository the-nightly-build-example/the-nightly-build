# researcher brief: the-instruments/bertscore (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, research directions, contradictions to probe, and source floor
- ../../editorial-direction.md — the citation standard, the series territory, and the declared reader

Output: evidence.md (in this directory)

Source floor: at least 8 sources, at least 4 primary, at least 1 secondary. The
BERTScore paper and the metric's own documentation are primary; a paper owning a
critique or comparison figure is primary for that figure. Verify every number
against its owning primary, confirm every URL resolves to the source's own page,
and find a concrete documented case where the number misled rather than a general
worry. Record contradictions (later studies where it is no better than cheaper or
learned metrics) in full.
