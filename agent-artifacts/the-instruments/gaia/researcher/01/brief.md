This round's focus: pin the numbers that build a GAIA score and the one
misleading episode.
  - From the GAIA paper: total question count and the split across the three
    levels; the exact grading rule (quasi-exact match against one reference); the
    reported human baseline and the release-time model scores; the validation/test
    design and whether test answers are withheld.
  - From the Hugging Face GAIA leaderboard/dataset pages: who runs it, how a score
    is submitted, and the current top entries (record the reading date).
  - The one real case where a GAIA figure misled: identify the specific
    system/report and the specific claim, verify the score against the primary
    that reported it, and record the concrete cost (a claim that did not transfer,
    a formatting mismatch, a "human-level" reading). Two retellings of one origin
    count as one; get an independent confirmation for any accusation of
    overfitting or gaming.
  - Contradictions in full: any source disputing that GAIA measures "general"
    ability, or defending a score the piece questions.
Confirm every leaderboard/dataset URL resolves to its own page, not a fetch
endpoint.
