# writing-coach brief: the-instruments/brier-score (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment: how the Brier score is computed, what it
  bundles and hides, and a real case where it misled people.
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, and the series prompt.

Output: ./voice-guide.md

This is an explanatory lesson taking apart a scoring metric for a smart,
widely-read reader with no time in a codebase, no code. Study writers who explain
a statistic and its failure modes with a clean worked example, who handle
probability honestly, and whose skepticism stays specific. Choose exemplars whose
craft carries over to explaining a measurement and where it deceives; do not pick
pieces that hand the writer this lesson's own findings.
