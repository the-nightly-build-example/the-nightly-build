# writing-coach brief: when-ai-breaks/retinopathy-field-study (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, and boundaries for this lesson
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop and headline standards, the paper's voice, the lesson template identity, and the series prompt

Output: voice-guide.md (in this directory)

Domain and genre: an explanatory lesson that tells one real deployment failure in
order and then teaches why that kind of system fails that way, for a smart reader
with no time in a codebase. Study writers who narrate a technical failure with
named people and dates and then extract the cause without melodrama or blame
theater. Quote passages that show clear chronological reporting and a calm move
from event to explanation; skip passages that only carry their own subject.

This round's focus: the piece must hold sympathy for the nurses and patients while
being precise that the model was accurate on the bench and still failed in the
clinic. Show voices that report a failure fairly and land the lesson in the facts.
