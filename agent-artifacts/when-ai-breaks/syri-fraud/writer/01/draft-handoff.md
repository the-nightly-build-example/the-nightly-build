# draft-handoff: when-ai-breaks/syri-fraud (01)

## Original work (one sentence)

This lesson turns the evidence's central absence into its argument: it shows that
a court struck SyRI down without proof of a single wrongful flag, argues that the
decisive failure was unfalsifiable opacity rather than a measured error (a secret,
rule-based risk model aimed at poor districts that no one, not even the court,
could open or test), and bridges that failure to present-day AI risk-scoring
without ever calling SyRI an AI model.

Where it is visible on the page: the "Why a secret score can't be proven wrong"
section, the pull quote ("No one ever showed that SyRI flagged the wrong
household, because no one was ever allowed to look"), and the AI bridge in "The
successor the Senate passed anyway."
