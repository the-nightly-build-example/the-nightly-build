Work from the record: the judgment, the legislation, the UN filings, and
reporting that held up. Where something you need is missing, ask me.

Source policy (when-ai-breaks): at least 8 sources, at least 4 primary, at least
1 secondary.

## Documents to read in full

1. The judgment: Rechtbank Den Haag, 5 February 2020, NJCM c.s. v. the State of
   the Netherlands. Find the ECLI number and the official English translation on
   rechtspraak.nl. Read it. Capture, with paragraph locators: what SyRI was and
   how it worked as the court describes it; the exact legal ground (Article 8
   ECHR, the fair-balance/proportionality test); what the court said about
   transparency and about the risk to poorer neighbourhoods; and exactly what it
   ordered. Record the court's own words where the wording is the evidence.
2. The SyRI legislation: the SUWI Act provisions and the Besluit SUWI that
   created SyRI (article numbers). Capture which bodies could participate and
   what data could be linked.
3. UN Special Rapporteur Philip Alston: the amicus curiae brief in the SyRI case
   (2019), and/or the 2019 report on the digital welfare state (UN doc
   A/74/493). Capture the specific criticisms he made of SyRI, with locators.

## Questions the record must answer

- Where SyRI was actually deployed (the specific municipalities/neighbourhoods),
  and confirmation that the targeted areas were low-income. Cite the document
  that owns each place name.
- Whether SyRI's risk model, indicators, or results were ever made public. This
  is central: establish, from the judgment or official sources, that the method
  and any error/false-positive rate were not disclosed.
- The timeline with dates: when SyRI was enacted, when projects ran, when the
  suit was filed, the ruling date, and what happened after (no appeal; the
  government halting it; the successor WGS / "Super SyRI" law and its status and
  criticism).
- The two sides of the dispute: the State's defence (necessity, proportionality,
  safeguards) in its own words, and the claimants'/court's case. Record both in
  full.
- The mechanism background needed to explain the failure: enough on base rates
  and false positives in mass fraud screening, and on proxy discrimination, that
  the writer can teach it accurately. A primary or strong scholarly source for
  the general statistical point is fine.

## Distinguish the neighbour

Confirm the factual difference between SyRI and the childcare-benefits scandal
(toeslagenaffaire): different system, different agency lead, different legal
event. Record one or two sourced sentences the writer can use to keep them
separate. Do not conflate the two in the record.

## Look for what breaks the angle

The angle is that opacity and proportionality, not a proven wrongful flag, were
the decisive failure. Search for: any evidence that SyRI did produce documented
wrongful outcomes (which would change the framing), and any credible defence that
the court got it wrong. Record contradictions in full.

## Source assets

Note any figure, map, or document detail worth showing (e.g., a map of targeted
neighbourhoods, or a quoted paragraph of the judgment). Give its location and
what a crop must keep. Do not prescribe crop coordinates.

Classify each source primary/secondary by authorship and stake. Record exact
names, roles, and affiliations as the primaries state them (the court's name, the
claimant organisations' full names, Alston's exact title). A wrong label reaches
the headline.
