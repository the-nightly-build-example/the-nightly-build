## Correct

Thesis: The Hague District Court stopped SyRI on 5 February 2020 without any
proof that it had ever flagged the wrong person, because the risk model was
secret and its accuracy could never be tested; the decisive failure was
unfalsifiable opacity, not a measured error. Claims under it: (1) SyRI was a
secret, rule-based automated risk score, never established as AI; (2) it was
struck under Article 8 ECHR as insufficiently transparent and verifiable, on a
risk of discrimination against poor and immigrant-background residents, not a
finding of realised discrimination; (3) the same failure shape now sits in
AI risk-scoring and the WGS successor. All three are stated cleanly from the
draft alone.

## Reads well

The draft ran clean in the middles; the work was at the edges. I cut the body's
last sentence, "That test now reaches well beyond one Dutch fraud tool." It is
an empty-conclusion flourish that survives the placeholder test ("That X now
reaches well beyond one Y"), it states nothing the preceding sentences had not
already established, and it duplicated the takeaway's "others like it." Removing
it lands the body on the court's own rule ("a risk model aimed at the poor has to
be transparent and verifiable enough to check, or it cannot stand"), a concrete
close of the kind the voice guide asks for.

I checked the opening bookend, every section's first and last sentence, and the
article's last sentence for slop and for briefing leaks. The Why-this-matters
opener states the general phenomenon and carries it straight to the SyRI
instance; it does not use the desk's "When you" or "You have probably" molds. No
sentence lifts clause order from commission.md's statement of the reader's
situation, and no clause is borrowed from the voice guide's exemplars (their
subjects are unrelated, and there is no lexical carryover). The antithesis
constructions that remain ("not, on the record, an artificial-intelligence
model"; "the shape of the failure, not the technology") each correct a real
misconception that the piece states, which is the one condition under which
`spec/slop.md` allows the form.

## The experience

Read from the top, the page moves from a specific flagged neighbourhood to the
secret model, the ruling, the mechanism, and the successor law, in the order the
lesson needs. The stat strip (6 bodies, 17 data categories, 5 projects), the two
court-language notes, the two position cards, and the pull quote each show
something faster than prose and each checks out against the record; none plots or
implies more than the source supports. No component is missing: the chronology
is light enough to carry in prose, so the timeline the writer considered and cut
was the right call.

What the piece gives beyond its sources: it turns the record's central absence
into the argument. A court struck a risk-scoring system down with no proof of a
single wrongful flag, and the piece shows why unfalsifiable opacity, not a
measured error, was the decisive failure, then bridges that to present-day AI
risk-scoring without ever calling SyRI AI. That is a reading of the judgment the
judgment does not state about itself, and it survives.
