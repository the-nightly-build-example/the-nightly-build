Domain and genre: an incident lesson about a product-safety failure that ended in
a death and a lawsuit, for a smart general reader. The register is sober, exact,
and restrained; it reports a tragedy without dramatizing it and explains the

product-safety failures with precision and restraint. Choose passages that show
how to write hard facts plainly; leave out any exemplar whose power comes from
