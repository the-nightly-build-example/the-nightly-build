## Original work

This article sets HireVue's two conflicting accounts of the face's weight
(2019: 10-30% / up to 29% of a score; 2021: about 0.25-4% of predictive power)
and the audit HireVue offered against Barrett et al., to show that the contest
over how much the face counted was beside the point because the score had no
valid construct to weigh in the first place. The evidence record carries these
claims separately; it does not draw that conclusion. The move is visible in the
"What a face cannot report" and "The face comes out in 2020" sections and the
weighting table.

## Open question (date framing)

The headline, dek, and body state the facial component was "dropped in 2020."
That rests on HireVue's own account of the timing ("early in 2020"), announced
January 12, 2021. The record contests HireVue's *motive*, not the *timing*, so
I treated the 2020 removal year as reported fact and attributed the motive
throughout as the company's framing (value, not conceded invalidity). No public
FTC enforcement outcome is in the record, so the piece implies no FTC order.
If editorial prefers the verified announcement date (Jan 2021) foregrounded in
display text instead of the company-stated 2020 removal, that is a one-line
change to headline/dek/subhead.
