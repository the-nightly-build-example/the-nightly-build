The article narrates a real deployed-AI failure and then explains why the
measurement was invalid, for a smart reader new to it. Study writers who tell an
accountability or science-and-society story with a clear timeline and then make the
underlying technical or scientific flaw legible (the kind of writer the field rates
for explaining what went wrong without either hype or cheap outrage). The craft
problem is holding a fair, evidence-led account while being precise that the
failure is one of construct validity, not just biased data. Do not let an exemplar
hand the writer this incident's own facts or conclusions.
