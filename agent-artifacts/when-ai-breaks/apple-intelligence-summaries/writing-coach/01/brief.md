Output: writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md (in this same artifact directory)

This series pins no voice guide, so calibrate from scratch per your skill. The
paper's register is a smart general reader with no time in a codebase, explained
to the way Matt Yglesias explains something he understands well: plain claims,
concrete stakes, no fuss, no hype or doom. The domain is narrating a deployed-
technology failure in order and then explaining its cause. Choose exemplars who
narrate a technical failure and its mechanism plainly and without moralizing;
keep any exemplar touching this incident to passages that show the writing, not
its findings.
