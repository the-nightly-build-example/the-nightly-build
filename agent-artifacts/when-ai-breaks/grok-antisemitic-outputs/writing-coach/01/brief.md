The domain is incident writing: a factual postmortem of a deployed system that
failed in public and did harm, told in order and from the record, explaining the
mechanism without sensationalizing it. The subject involves hateful content, so
the model to study is measured, precise incident and accountability reporting
that stays clinical about ugly material and keeps the reader's attention on cause
and record, never on spectacle. Study writers who do calm, exact postmortems of
technology failures. The register is serious and restrained, no hype and no doom.
