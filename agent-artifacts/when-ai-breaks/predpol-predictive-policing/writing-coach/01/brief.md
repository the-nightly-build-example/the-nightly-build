This lesson narrates a real failure of a predictive-policing system and teaches why
it broke, for a smart, widely-read reader with no time in a codebase, no code.
Study writers who tell an investigative/technical post-mortem clearly, keep the
numbers honest, and explain a system's failure without sloganeering — the harm is
real, but the lesson is the mechanism. Choose exemplars whose craft carries over to
a data-driven accountability narrative and its mechanism; do not pick pieces that
supply this incident's own explanation.
