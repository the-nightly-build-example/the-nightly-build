  sensitivity note, and habits not to inherit.
- `editorial-direction.md` (at the artifact root) — house standard, slop, headline

Output: `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md` (under this article's artifact root).

This round: the genre is an incident lesson that tells a deployed system's public
failure in order, then pulls one transferable mechanism from it, for a smart
reader new to how such systems break. The subject is sensitive (harm to people
with eating disorders), so study writers who report harm to vulnerable people with
restraint and precision: who name people, dates, and facts, keep the sequence
honest, and land the lesson without sensationalizing or moralizing. Prefer
explanatory reporters who stay calm and concrete over dramatic feature writers.

Break the paper-wide opener formula ("By the end you will know X. You will also
see Y") and do not model when-ai-breaks' recurring "where the same weakness runs
now" closer or its single-vivid-label orientation.

Run environment: outbound web is available through a proxy. Retrieve the raw text
of every exemplar page and verify each quotation word for word against it before
reporting, per your skill.
