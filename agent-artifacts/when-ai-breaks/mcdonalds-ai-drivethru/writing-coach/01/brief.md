## What this piece is
A lesson that reconstructs a real, somewhat comic deployment failure (McDonald's
AI drive-thru) in order, then uses it to teach why voice order-taking is a hard
machine problem. Domain: a technology-failure incident with a corporate and
engineering record. Genre: narrative-then-explanation — tell what happened
plainly with names and dates, then turn it into a mechanism the reader keeps.

## What the voice guide is for
Study writers who report a technical or institutional failure cleanly: who tell
the sequence with named actors and dates, resist both mockery and excuse, and
pull a real lesson out of an episode that was funny in the news. The best
exemplars here handle post-mortems, incident reporting, or business-technology
narrative and stay precise about cause. The subject invites a cheap laugh; the
guide should point the writer toward wit anchored in the story's own facts, with
the explanation carrying the weight.
