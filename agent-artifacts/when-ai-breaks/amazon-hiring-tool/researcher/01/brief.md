Source floor: >= 8 sources, >= 4 primary, >= 1 secondary. The origin account is
one investigation; do not let retellings masquerade as independent confirmation.

Questions the evidence must answer:
1. The primary account: the 2018 Reuters investigation (Jeffrey Dastin, "Amazon
   scraps secret AI recruiting tool that showed bias against women"). Record
   exactly what it reports and how it is sourced (people familiar with the
   project, anonymous). Get the specifics firsthand: timeline (built ~2014, team
   size, ~500 models, scored 1–5 stars, trained on ~10 years of resumes), the
   concrete bias behavior (penalized "women's," downgraded two all-women's
   colleges), the neutralization attempts, that the ratings were never the sole
   basis for hiring, and that the project was disbanded by ~2017–2018. Quote the
   exact wording where it is itself evidence.
2. Any Amazon statement or acknowledgment (to Reuters or elsewhere), and its
   exact wording. Distinguish what Amazon confirmed from what only the sources
   assert.
3. Independent corroboration: is there any second, independent source in a
   position to know (not a retelling of Reuters)? If none exists, say so plainly
   and mark the account as single-origin — that limitation is itself important.
4. The mechanism, from the fairness literature (primary/peer-reviewed): proxy
   discrimination / redundant encoding — that removing a protected attribute
   does not remove bias because models reconstruct it from correlated features.
   Get a citable source that owns this result (e.g. foundational algorithmic-
   fairness papers). Also the general point that a model trained on past
   decisions reproduces their bias.
5. Where the weakness lives today: the text and status of NYC Local Law 144
   (automated employment decision tools bias audits) and its rules — primary;
   EEOC guidance on AI in hiring — primary; and evidence that automated resume
   screening is now widespread. (Note: the paper has a published lesson at
   ai-in-the-world/nyc-hiring-bias-audits the writer may link; you do not need to
   re-derive the law, but confirm the citable primary text exists and resolves.)

Contradictions/limits to record: the harm is bounded (experimental tool, never
the sole screen) — do not let the evidence support a claim that it rejected
applicants at scale. Record what is confirmed vs single-sourced. In Numbers,
record team size, model count, star scale, training-window years, and any Local
Law 144 dates/thresholds, each with its owning source.
