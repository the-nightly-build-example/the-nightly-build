# Evidence record: when-ai-breaks/clearview-ai (02) — single-fact supplement

This record supplements record 01, which stands unchanged. It resolves one open
question the editor flagged: the article states Clearview "has since been granted
permission to appeal once more, to the Court of Appeal," carried in the draft by
citation 9 (the Upper Tribunal decision page), which does not contain that fact.
The fact is real and is now sourced to an openable page: a Clearview appeal to the
Court of Appeal was granted on 19 December 2025, following the Upper Tribunal's 6
October 2025 judgment for the ICO. The only openable source I could confirm is
secondary (a UK law firm's case note); the ICO's own news pages remain gated to my
fetcher (403), as recorded in 01. One caveat for the writer: this source reports
the grant as a procedural fact and does not attribute it to the Commissioner, so
the draft's "According to the Commissioner" framing is not what the cited source
carries — state it as reported, or drop the attribution.

## Contradictions

None on this fact. The page does not name which body granted permission (Upper
Tribunal or Court of Appeal itself); the 19 December 2025 date and the fact of the
grant are consistent across the search results and uncontested. One framing
mismatch, not a factual contradiction: the draft attributes the grant to the
Commissioner ("According to the Commissioner"), but this source reports it as a
neutral procedural fact and makes no such attribution — the writer should not cite
this source for a "Commissioner said" clause.
