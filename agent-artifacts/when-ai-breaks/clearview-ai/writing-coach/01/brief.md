This article: a lesson that tells one AI failure from the record — Clearview AI
scraped billions of web photos to build a police face-search engine and was found
unlawful by regulators and courts in several countries — then explains why that
kind of system fails and where scraped facial recognition persists. The reader is
smart, widely read, new to both the technology and the privacy-law frame. The
craft challenge is precise investigative/regulatory narrative built from the
record's own dates, figures, and rulings, staying factual and never
sensationalizing, plus rendering multi-country legal findings in plain words.

Study writers the field rates for accurate investigative and regulatory reporting
and for explaining legal/privacy rulings to lay readers — clear explanatory-
journalism and legal-explainer writers. At least three exemplars, read in full,
quoted for how they build a factual sequence from records and how they render a
ruling in plain words without losing precision. Quote for the writing, not for
Clearview facts.
