This round's focus: the piece narrates two deaths and a regulatory fight without
melodrama and without going clinical — reported, exact, humane. Study writers who
handle accident investigations or safety failures: who can lay out a sequence of
events and a technical cause plainly, hold two disputing accounts side by side
fairly, and let the record carry the weight instead of adjectives. Prefer
investigative or explanatory writers on engineering failure and safety; avoid
anyone whose register is outrage or advocacy. The subject involves real people
who died — the exemplars should show restraint that respects that while staying
precise.
