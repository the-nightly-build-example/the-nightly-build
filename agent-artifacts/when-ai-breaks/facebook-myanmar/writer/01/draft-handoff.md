# Draft handoff: when-ai-breaks/facebook-myanmar (01)

## Original work

The piece turns a record that runs from Amnesty's "proactively amplified" to
BSR's "not fully understood" into a single sized account: it teaches the
settled mechanism (an engagement-ranked feed amplifying incitement that
near-absent Burmese-language moderation left unchecked) as ordinary and
as-designed, holds it apart from the contested *magnitude* of Facebook's
contribution, and names the exact missing data — Myanmar-specific distribution
telemetry plus a counterfactual — that would be needed to settle that magnitude
and does not exist. The apportioning (military planned and drove; platform
amplified and failed to moderate) is carried as the grammar of the whole lesson,
not a one-line disclaimer.

## Restraint / proportion decisions the editor should know

- **No human particular used.** The voice guide (license 1) permitted one
  sourced, mechanism-serving human particular and left open whether the opening
  bookend could carry one. I used none. The abstraction is made observable
  through scale facts already in the record (≈20M users for whom "Facebook is
  the Internet"; ~1 → ~60 → 99 Burmese reviewers; nearly 725,000 fled) rather
  than a named person, which keeps the "never lurid / no described injury"
  constraint absolute. If the editor wants the Taub/Fisher-style single named
  scene, that is a deliberate add, not a gap I wrote around.
- **"Proactively amplified" appears only as Amnesty's conclusion**, in the
  record-and-limits section, immediately caveated that it rests on general
  Facebook-Papers evidence plus inference, not Myanmar telemetry. The dek's
  "amplified anti-Rohingya incitement" is the record-supported synthesis (per
  the brief), sized in the same sentence by "a campaign the Tatmadaw planned and
  carried out." The headline claims only what is established (ranked for
  engagement; could not moderate Burmese).
- **~2% hate-speech enforcement** is presented as platform-wide (internal Meta,
  2019, via Amnesty), explicitly not Myanmar-specific.
- The moderation table's caption states the buildup postdates the August 2017
  campaign, so the "improving" trend cannot read as pre-atrocity diligence.
