Focus: the genre is an incident lesson that reconstructs a systemic technical
failure from the record and teaches why that kind of system fails that way. The
record includes a mass atrocity; the prose must be exact and unsentimental, never
lurid, never minimizing, and proportionate about responsibility (a platform
amplified and failed to moderate; it did not commit the violence). Study writers
who narrate technology's role in grave harm from primary investigation documents
with restraint and precision, keeping causal claims sized to the evidence.
Calibrate craft for reporting that carries human stakes through exact facts, not
adjectives, and turns a human-rights record into a mechanism lesson without
editorializing or overclaiming.
