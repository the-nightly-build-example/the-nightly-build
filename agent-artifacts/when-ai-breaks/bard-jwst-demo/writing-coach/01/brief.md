The article is a lesson that tells one AI failure — Google's Bard launch demo
stating a false fact about the James Webb telescope — in order, then explains why
the technology fails that way, for a smart non-technical reader. Study writers who
narrate a technical incident with restraint and precision: the sequence of events
kept factual and dated, the mechanism explained plainly, no melodrama and no
easy mockery. That controlled incident-narration register is the craft problem;
the paper voice forbids hype and doom alike.
