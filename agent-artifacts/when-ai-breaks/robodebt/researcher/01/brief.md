- The Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme, Final Report (2023).
- The Federal Court judgment (the Amato case and/or the class-action settlement approval).
- The Commonwealth Ombudsman's report on the Online Compliance Intervention.
- A government or ANAO document on the scheme's operation or the settlement figures.

Answer and verify against the owning primary, with figures and their scope:
- How the scheme worked mechanically: the data match between Centrelink and Australian Taxation Office
  records, and the income-averaging step that spread annual income evenly across fortnights. State exactly
  what the average was computed from and what it was treated as proving.
- The reversal of the burden of proof: what recipients were required to do, and what changed from the prior
  process (including whether a human check was removed). Cite the finding.
- Every hard figure against the primary that owns it: the number of debts raised, the number of people
  affected, the total dollar value, and the class-action settlement amount (and what it covered). Note any
  figure that only appears in reporting.
- The lawfulness finding: what the Federal Court and the Royal Commission concluded, in their words.
- Human harm: record only what the Royal Commission, court, or Ombudsman establish, with their exact
  characterization. Flag anything that is contested or that reporting asserts beyond the official findings;
  do not state a causal harm the record does not support.
- For the where-it-lives-today close: a sourced, factual note on a comparable automated eligibility or
  data-matching system, without editorial reach.

Record contradictions in full, and separate official findings from reporting throughout. Confirm every URL
resolves to the document's own page.
