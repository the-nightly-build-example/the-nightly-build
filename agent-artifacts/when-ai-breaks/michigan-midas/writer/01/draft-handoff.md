# draft-handoff: when-ai-breaks/michigan-midas (01)

## Original work

The article turns the evidence record's tangle of competing figures and
mis-attributed reviews into one ordered lesson: it keeps the December 2016 and
August 2017 state reviews strictly apart so every rate carries its own
denominator, period, and owner, and it isolates the removal of the human
examiner (not the data-matching) as the specific step that converted a modest
false-positive rate into mass harm — a causal argument the evidence supplies the
parts for but never assembles.
