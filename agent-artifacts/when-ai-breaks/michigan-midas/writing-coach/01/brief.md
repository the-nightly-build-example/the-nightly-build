(Michigan's MiDAS falsely accusing jobless people of fraud) from the record —
what it did, who it hit, how it ended — then teaches why that kind of system

without melodrama. The register is plain, factual, and humane (house touchstone:
Matt Yglesias; the series wants the record to carry the weight, no hype and no
doom). Favor exemplars strong at moving from a human scene to the mechanism
