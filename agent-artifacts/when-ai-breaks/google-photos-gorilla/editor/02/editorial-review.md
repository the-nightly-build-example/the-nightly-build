## Skeptic

The two added links carry claims about other systems, so I tested them against
the sibling articles they point to.

- **rite-aid link.** "It is the same demographic-error gap behind Rite Aid's
  store-camera facial recognition." Placed at the peak of the demographic-error
  discussion, right after the Gender Shades intersection sentence — the natural
  first mention. I opened the target: it exists in the published library and
  rests squarely on a demographic false-match gap in facial recognition,
  differential by group and anchored to NIST NISTIR 8280 ("demographic error
  rates the government had already measured"). "The same demographic-error gap"
  is accurate and does not overclaim; the phrasing is general enough to hold
  across a gender-classification accuracy gap and a facial-recognition false-
  match gap, and the sibling's own text uses "demographic error rates." It names
  no claim this piece cannot support and does not re-teach Rite Aid.
- **gemini link.** "An image generator can miss the other way: Google's Gemini
  image generator overcorrected for under-representation and drew historically
  false pictures, where this system left a gap open." Placed in the closing body
  paragraph, the piece's only touch on image generation — a natural first
  mention. I opened the target: its dek confirms "a rule meant to diversify
  Gemini's people pictures fired on every prompt, even ones history had already
  answered," i.e. a generation overcorrection producing historically false
  images. The distinction is drawn correctly: that case is generation
  overcorrection, this one a classification failure from under-representation.
  No claim the piece cannot support.

Both link sentences carry content, not filler: the rite-aid line makes a claim
(the same gap in another system); the gemini line carries a reasoning step (the
mechanism can fail in the opposite direction) plus a fact about that case.
Neither is a signpost or self-grade, and both are grammatically clean.

The two redundancy trims (a mechanism restatement in the bounding paragraph and
two filler phrases, per the handoff) removed nothing load-bearing. The bounding

keeps its caveats and its 6.2% figure. No fact, disputable claim, or reasoning
step was lost, and the word band is satisfied (2200, top of the 1200–2200 band).
