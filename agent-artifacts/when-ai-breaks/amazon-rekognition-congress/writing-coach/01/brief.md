This round's focus: an explanatory lesson that narrates one real incident in
order and then teaches the mechanism behind it, naming people, companies, and
dates. Study writers who tell a technical failure or accountability story
clearly and fairly to a general reader, without melodrama. Exemplars need not be
about AI. Avoid exemplars whose subject is Rekognition or facial recognition, so
no finding leaks into the guide.
