## What this piece is
A lesson that teaches one primary research paper — the 2020 "Denoising Diffusion
Probabilistic Models" paper — to a smart, widely read reader with no machine
learning background. Domain: technical explanation of a machine learning method
and its history. Genre: explanatory writing that walks a reader through a real
method and its numbers, then corrects a common misconception (that "diffusion
model" means what the 2020 paper built).

## What the voice guide is for
Study writers who explain a technical mechanism to a general but serious reader
without dumbing it down, and who can walk through real numbers and a method
plainly. The best exemplars for this piece explain how a system actually works
and are honest about what a result does and does not show. Find them among
writers the field itself rates for technical explanation (for example, the kind
of clear ML/systems explainer the press names in its voice — plain claims,
concrete stakes, no fuss).

An exemplar that itself explains diffusion models needs care: quote it only where
the passage shows the writing and not the diffusion findings this article will
report, or choose a different piece, so the writer inherits craft and not
content.
