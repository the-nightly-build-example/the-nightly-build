## The two sharpening findings

- **15.3% vs 18.2%:** handled in the orientation section and its table. The
  round 15.3% is attributed to the seven-net ensemble; the single model's 18.2%
  (validation) is credited as still ~8 points ahead of the 26.2% runner-up. The
  table labels validation vs test scope per row so the single/ensemble mix is
  not read as one measurement.
- **Pre-AlexNet GPU-CNN wins:** the "AlexNet stood on ImageNet and DanNet"
  section carries Ciresan et al. (nicknamed DanNet) as GPU-trained deep CNNs
  already winning vision contests and beating people on traffic signs, cited by
  AlexNet itself — framed as the method predating the paper, not an invention
  story. The three "superseded" claims are each pinned to their own primary (BN
  retiring LRN; ViT challenging the convolutional form; Recht on generalization)
  and none is stated as a universal verdict.
