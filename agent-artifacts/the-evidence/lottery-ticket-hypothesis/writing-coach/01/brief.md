learning paper back against what it actually showed, for a smart reader new to the
subject with no time in a codebase. Study writers the field rates for turning a
result into something a careful lay reader can weigh: the best explainers of ML
and science who state a striking claim, size the experiment honestly, and keep
their own judgment visible without hype. Prefer writers who make a technical
result concrete over popularizers who simplify past the evidence.

see Y"): do not model it, and point any Why-this-matters direction elsewhere.
