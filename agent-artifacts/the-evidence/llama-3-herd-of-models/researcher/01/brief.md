# researcher brief: the-evidence/llama-3-herd-of-models (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- ../../commission.md — subject, angle, and source obligations

Output: evidence.md

Central primary is the report itself, "The Llama 3 Herd of Models" (Meta AI,
arXiv:2407.21783, July 2024). Read the paper, not coverage of it. Establish
firsthand, with locators:
- The models released and their sizes (8B, 70B, 405B), and the architecture
  (dense transformer, context length, tokenizer/vocab), including what changed
  from Llama 2.
- Training-data scale (token count of the pretraining corpus) and what the
  report does and does not disclose about that data.
- Training compute for the 405B (FLOPs and/or GPU-hours and hardware), stated as
  the report states it.
- The report's own benchmark table: the exact figures it reports for Llama 3
  405B against GPT-4, GPT-4o, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on named benchmarks, with
  the evals named. Record which comparisons the report runs and note any obvious
  gaps.
- Any contamination/decontamination or evaluation-methodology section, quoted
  where the wording matters.
Further primaries for what "open" means: the Llama 3 model card and the Llama 3
Community License (Meta), for the license's use restrictions and what is and is
not distributed (weights vs. training data vs. training code).
Secondary (at least one, from outside Meta): independent reporting on the release
or an independent benchmark/leaderboard or a benchmark-contamination source that
bears on how much weight the self-reported table carries. A repetition supports
only that a claim was made.
Search for what breaks the angle: independent results that diverge from Meta's
table, criticism of the "open" label, or contamination concerns. Record
contradictions in full. Meet the counts (min 6; primary >=3; secondary >=1) with
sources that change the interpretation, never padding. Confirm every URL
resolves to the document's own page.
