Subject: a lesson reading the 2023 Microsoft paper "Textbooks Are All You Need"
(the phi-1 code model), for a smart non-practitioner. The piece explains what the
paper actually did with its data and numbers, and separates the earned finding
from the "quality beats scale" slogan the title became.

The genre is expository science writing that reads a technical document closely
and holds its claims to the evidence. Study writers who explain a machine-learning
result to a general but serious reader without hype and without dumbing the
technical content down, and who are precise about how big a result really is. Good
exemplar pools: careful ML explainers and critics who quantify claims. Choose
exemplars for how they sound, not for their subject; if an exemplar discusses
