# writing-coach brief: the-evidence/gpt-2 (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, this paper's voice, the lesson
  template identity, and the series prompt.
- ../../commission.md — the subject, the angle, and this desk's job.

Output: ./voice-guide.md

Genre and domain: an explanatory lesson that reads one famous AI research
document (the 2019 GPT-2 paper) for a smart, widely read reader with no time in a
codebase. The voice is plain, load-bearing explanation of a technical primary
source and the public story around it, in the register of a writer who explains
something they understand well without fuss or hype. Study exemplars who explain
machine-learning or technical research to a general-but-serious audience and make
a document's real content legible; pick passages that show that, not passages
carrying GPT-2's own findings.

Run environment: web tools work through a configured proxy. Read each exemplar in
full from its own page and verify every quotation word for word before reporting.
