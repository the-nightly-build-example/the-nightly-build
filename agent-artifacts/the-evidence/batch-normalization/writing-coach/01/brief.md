# writing-coach brief: the-evidence/batch-normalization (01)

Inputs:
- `../../commission.md` — the assignment: read the batch-norm paper and separate
  the indispensable technique from its stated-but-wrong "internal covariate shift"
  explanation.
- `../../editorial-direction.md` — house standard, the paper's voice, series prompt.

Output: `voice-guide.md` (this directory)

This round's focus: the piece explains a widely believed explanation, then shows a
controlled experiment overturning it — without either mystifying the technique or
sneering at the original authors. Study writers who can say "everyone believes X
is why this works, and here is the experiment showing it is not" in plain prose,
who respect a result while correcting its interpretation, and who make a training
mechanism concrete. Prefer explainers of science or engineering who handle "the
textbook reason is wrong" with generosity and precision. Choose exemplars on other
subjects so their material cannot leak into this piece's own findings.
