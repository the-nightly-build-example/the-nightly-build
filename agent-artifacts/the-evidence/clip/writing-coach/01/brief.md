# writing-coach brief: the-evidence/clip (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (at the artifact root): the house standard, the
  paper's voice, the lesson template's teaching rules, and the-evidence
  series prompt.

Output: agent-artifacts/the-evidence/clip/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

The article: a lesson that reads one famous machine-learning paper (OpenAI's
2021 CLIP paper) for a smart, non-technical reader, showing what the paper
actually measured versus how its "zero-shot" result is invoked today. Genre:
explanatory paper-reading for a serious general audience. Length band
1200-2200 words.

This round's focus: study writers who explain a technical research result to
non-specialists without dumbing it down or hyping it, and who are good at
separating what a paper showed from what people later claimed it showed.
Exemplars should not be about CLIP itself; if one is, quote only where the
passage shows the writing and not CLIP's findings.
