This article: a lesson that reads the sparse mixture-of-experts paper (Shazeer et
al. 2017) and shows what routing tokens to a few experts really does, the honest
scale of the original result, and why a MoE model's advertised parameter count
overstates what runs per token. The reader is smart, widely read, new to neural-
network architecture. The craft challenge is explaining a routing mechanism and a
counterintuitive size/compute decoupling in plain words, and puncturing an
inflated headline number without contempt.

Study writers the field rates for making a technical architecture legible to a
lay-but-smart reader, and for turning a big number into an honest one. At least
three exemplars, read in full, quoted for how they build a mechanism from a
concrete case and how they correct a misleading figure evenly. Quote for the
writing, not for MoE facts.
