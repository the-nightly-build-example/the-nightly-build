# writing-coach brief: the-evidence/flamingo (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment: read the 2022 Flamingo paper as a
  document, its real few-shot multimodal claim and scale, and how it is used today.
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, and the series prompt.

Output: ./voice-guide.md

This is an explanatory lesson teaching a landmark machine-learning paper — a
few-shot multimodal (vision + language) model — to a smart, widely-read reader
with no time in a codebase, no code. Study writers who make a deep model design
and its results legible without hype or hand-waving, and who report what a system
can and cannot do with honest scope. Choose exemplars whose craft carries over to
explaining an architecture and a benchmark result plainly; do not choose pieces
that hand the writer this paper's own framings.
