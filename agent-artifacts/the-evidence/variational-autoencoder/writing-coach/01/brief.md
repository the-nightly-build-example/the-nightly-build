The article explains a foundational generative-model paper to a smart reader new
to it. Study writers who make a probabilistic or deep-learning method legible to
non-specialists without an equation dump and without talking down (the kind of
technical explainer the field rates). The hardest craft problem is teaching the
ELBO and the reparameterization trick in plain words with a worked example, and
being honest that a famous method paper was a small-scale proof. Do not let any
exemplar hand the writer this paper's own findings or framings.
