conditions behind it for a smart general reader. Study writers who explain a
technical achievement precisely and without hype, separating what was shown from
how it is remembered. Exemplars need not be about AI or games. Avoid exemplars
whose subject is AlphaStar, StarCraft, or game-playing AI, so no finding leaks into
the guide.
