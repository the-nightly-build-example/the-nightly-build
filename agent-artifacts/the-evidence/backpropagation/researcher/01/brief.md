# researcher brief: the-evidence/backpropagation (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- ../../commission.md — the subject, angle, required contribution, and source floor
Output: ./evidence.md

This round's focus, from the commission: build the evidence to (1) state what the
1986 Nature paper and its PDP-chapter companion actually did — the learning rule,
the specific experiments and tasks, the network sizes, the training effort, and
the numbers the authors reported — and (2) establish the priority record from the
predecessor primaries the commission names, including where the 1986 authors
themselves credited earlier work, and (3) supply the later primary result on the
local-minima/saddle-point question so the correction is sourced. Read the actual
papers and the exact passages, not retrospectives, for every load-bearing figure;
retrospectives are secondary context only.

Run-environment note: outbound HTTPS is proxied. Historical papers often sit
behind publisher paywalls or as scanned PDFs; a 403 or paywall is gated, not
dead. Try a browser-style fetch and record the document's own canonical page as
the URL, not the fetch route.
