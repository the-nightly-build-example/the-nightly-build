Output: ./voice-guide.md (beside this brief)

This lesson explains a technical AI document (speech recognition) to a smart
reader new to the field, in the paper's plain, concrete register. Study writers
who explain a measurement-heavy engineering result without hype and without
talking down: people who can define word error rate or weak supervision in a
sentence and keep a human on the page. Speech/ML explainer writing and rigorous
science journalism are the domains; pick exemplars whose voice already sounds the
way this piece should. Do not quote passages that hand the writer Whisper's own
findings.
