# writing-coach brief: the-evidence/mamba (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, and boundaries for this lesson
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop and headline standards, the paper's voice, the lesson template identity, and the series prompt

Output: voice-guide.md (in this directory)

Domain and genre: an explanatory lesson that reads one technical AI research
paper (a machine-learning architecture) for a smart reader who has no time in a
codebase. Study writers who explain hard machine-learning ideas in plain,
confident prose without hype. Quote passages that show how to keep a technical
explanation concrete and honest about scale; leave out any passage whose value is
its own subject matter, since the writer inherits whatever is in the guide.

This round's focus: the piece must teach a state-space mechanism to a reader who
knows attention but not this, and must stay skeptical about the "transformers are
over" framing. Show the writer voices that carry a mechanism and a caveat in the
same breath.
