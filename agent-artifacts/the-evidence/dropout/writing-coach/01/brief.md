This is an explanatory lesson that reads one machine-learning technique paper for
a smart reader new to it. Study writers who explain a technical method plainly
and then hold its stated justification to what the evidence actually supports.
The piece teaches a training mechanism and then draws an honest line between a
technique's original reputation and its narrowed present-day role, so exemplars
that combine clear mechanism-teaching with skeptical calibration are worth most.
Choose passages that show explanation and honest assessment of a claim, not
passages that carry claims about dropout, regularization, or neural networks
themselves.
