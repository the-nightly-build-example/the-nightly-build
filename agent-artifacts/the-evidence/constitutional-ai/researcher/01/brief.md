# researcher brief: the-evidence/constitutional-ai (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — citation standard,
  series territory, declared reader
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the document, the angle, the source floor

Output: evidence.md (this directory)

Read the Constitutional AI paper (arXiv 2212.08073) firsthand and build the
evidence record the writer and editor will work from. Answer, from the sources
themselves:

- The two training stages, each component named, and what the paper reports the
  method achieved and how it was measured (model sizes, evaluation setup,
  Elo/preference numbers, red-team results). Get the exact figures and their
  scope from the paper.
- The constitution: how many principles, their stated provenance, and the exact
  wording of one or two, quoted, so the writer can show what a principle is.
- The RLHF baseline it builds on (companion Anthropic paper 2204.05862): what it
  supplies, so the writer can link rather than re-teach.
- How the method is used and described today, and what later work found about
  whether AI feedback matches human feedback (e.g. Lee et al., "RLAIF,"
  2309.00267) and Anthropic's Collective Constitutional AI. Record where present-
  day usage overstates the 2022 result.

Source floor: at least 6 sources, at least 3 primary, at least 1 secondary.
Classify each and say why. Record contradictions (e.g. RLAIF-vs-RLHF findings
that cut against the paper's framing) in full. Confirm every URL resolves to the
document's own page.
