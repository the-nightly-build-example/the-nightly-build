## Domain and genre
Mechanistic explanation of a machine-learning behavior for a smart lay reader:
starting from something the reader has felt (a refused harmless request) and
tracing it down to cause. Genre: the "how does it actually do that" explainer
that names real components and stops at bedrock. Study writers the field rates
for making model internals and training legible without code or hand-waving
(the tradition of clear interpretability/deep-learning explainers). Pick your
own ≥3 exemplars; prefer primary pieces; skip SEO/influencer content; no company
blog as a voice exemplar.

## What the voice guide must solve for this article
- Turning an internal, abstract finding (refusal as a single direction in the
  activations) into something concrete a reader can picture, without a diagram.
- Staging a causal chain (observed behavior → training → internal switch) so
  each step is visibly earned and the "we don't know" steps are marked plainly.
- Explaining a trained tendency without implying the model "decides" or
  "understands" harm; keep agency language honest.
- Correcting the intuitive misreading ("it judged my request dangerous")
  through fact, not a "not X but Y" reflex.
