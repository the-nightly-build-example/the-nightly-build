This is a mechanism lesson: it walks a smart general reader backward from a
familiar chatbot behavior to the training objective that produces it, step by
step, no code. The craft challenge is keeping each causal step concrete and
honest about what is settled versus open, without hedging theatrically. Study
writers who explain how a system works from the behavior down and who make
probability and language models intuitive to non-specialists. Show the writing,
not this article's own explanation of false premises.
