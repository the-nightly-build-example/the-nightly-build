# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/speech-to-text-hallucination (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, and boundaries for this lesson
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop and headline standards, the paper's voice, the lesson template identity, and the series prompt

Output: voice-guide.md (in this directory)

Domain and genre: an explanatory lesson that works backward from a surprising
machine behavior to its cause, for a smart reader with no time in a codebase.
Study writers who explain how a system actually works step by step, in plain
words, and who are careful to mark what is settled and what is still open. Quote
passages that show a mechanism being built up concretely; skip passages that only
carry their own subject.

This round's focus: the piece must make a fluent invention over silence feel
inevitable once the reader sees the decoder is a language model, without hype and
without turning a real harm into a scare. Show voices that explain a cause plainly
and hold the line between what is known and what is guessed.
