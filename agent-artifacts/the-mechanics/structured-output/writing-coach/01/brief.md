# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/structured-output (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, press voice, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the assignment: the behavior, the causal chain to walk, boundaries, and habits not to inherit

Output: voice-guide.md (this directory)

This piece starts from an everyday developer-facing behavior (asking for strict
JSON makes answers worse) and works down to its cause. Study writers who explain
a computational mechanism step by step and keep a worked example concrete. An
exemplar on constrained decoding or format restriction specifically will leak
the piece's own explanations, so quote it only where the passage shows craft.
