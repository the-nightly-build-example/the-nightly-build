# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/counting-objects-in-images (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — house standard, paper
  voice, series prompt, lesson template identity
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the behavior, angle, reader, boundaries

Output: voice-guide.md (this directory)

The article is a lesson that works backward from a familiar behavior (image
generators can't draw an exact number of things) to its cause, in plain words and
no code, for a smart non-technical reader. Study writers who explain how a system
works by walking the causal chain step by step and stopping at bedrock — the
register the paper voice describes. The craft problem is keeping a multi-step
mechanism (text encoder, diffusion, cross-attention, training statistics) concrete
and ordered so each step earns the next, without letting it turn into a parts
list.
