This lesson traces an everyday behavior of reasoning models down to its cause,
step by step, and marks where the explanation is settled versus still argued.
Study writers who explain how a system produces a counterintuitive behavior to a
curious non-specialist, taking the reader down one real layer at a time and
resisting a tidy just-so story. Value writers who can explain an incentive or a
training process concretely and stay honest about what remains unsettled. Choose
passages that show layered mechanical explanation and disciplined uncertainty,
not passages that carry claims about reasoning models, test-time compute, or
overthinking themselves.
