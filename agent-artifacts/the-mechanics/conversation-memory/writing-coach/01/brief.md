# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/conversation-memory (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, this paper's voice, the lesson
  template identity, and the series prompt.
- ../../commission.md — the behavior, the angle, and this desk's job.

Output: ./voice-guide.md

Genre and domain: an explanatory lesson that answers "how does a chatbot remember
what I said earlier," working backward from a behavior everyone has seen to the
plain mechanism (the model is stateless; the app resends the transcript). The
voice is calm, concrete, mechanism-first explanation that overturns an intuition
without theatrics and uses small worked examples. Study exemplars who explain how
a system actually works to a curious lay reader, step by step from a visible
behavior; pick passages that show that method, not passages carrying this lesson's
own mechanism.

Run environment: web tools work through a configured proxy. Read each exemplar in
full from its own page and verify every quotation word for word before reporting.
