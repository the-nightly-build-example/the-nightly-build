# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/hangman (01)

Inputs: editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md), commission.md (../../commission.md)
Output: ./voice-guide.md

Do not use the repository or prior articles for voice.

Genre: a plain-language explainer that starts from a funny, reproducible chatbot
behavior (it "cheats" at hangman) and works backward to a clean cause (it has no
hidden state). Register: Matt Yglesias explaining something he understands —
plain and concrete; the pleasure is the "oh, that's why," a light touch is
welcome but never at the cost of the mechanism.

Find at least three exemplars the field rates who explain a surprising computer
behavior down to its cause for a smart general reader, with a bit of wit:
candidates include Julia Evans, Simon Willison, Andrej Karpathy, Dan Luu. Prefer
writers who make a mechanism click with a small concrete example. If you quote a
piece on chatbot statelessness or hangman itself, quote for the writing only.

Two or three passages per exemplar, each from a different part, quoted exactly,
with checked URL and date. Under each, two or three plain sentences on what is
good and where the person shows. Report the path and the single strongest
takeaway for the writer.
