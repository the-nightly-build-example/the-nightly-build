# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/memorization (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, lesson identity, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the exact subject, angle, and required contribution

Output: ./voice-guide.md

Focus: the genre is a mechanism lesson that starts from a behavior everyone has
seen (a chatbot quoting a famous passage word for word) and walks down to a real
system fact, in plain words, no code. Study writers who explain how a computing
or statistical system actually works to a curious non-programmer, making an
internal fact (a string stored in the weights) vivid with a concrete example and
real numbers. Calibrate craft for prose that keeps one example alive across the
descent and cashes out every comparison into literal mechanism.
