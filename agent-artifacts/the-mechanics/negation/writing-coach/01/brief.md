This series pins no voice guide, so study the domain fresh. The article is an
explanatory lesson that starts from a familiar failure (a model ignoring "no"/
"don't") and works backward to its cause, for a smart reader with no
machine-learning background and no time in a codebase. Study writers who are
excellent at mechanistic explanation of everyday computing behavior: people who
start from something the reader has seen, then take it apart one honest step at a
time, using small concrete examples and admitting where the explanation stops.
Prefer writers technical readers rate for clarity about how systems actually
work. The craft problem specific to this piece: it must stay concrete (real
prompts and their wrong outputs) while covering two different systems (text and
image generation) and marking the boundary between settled mechanism and open
question without hedging everything. Find passages that move from a concrete
symptom to a real cause cleanly.
