The article works backward from a visible model behavior (position in a long input
changing whether a fact is used) to its cause, for a smart reader new to it, with
no code. Study writers who explain a system's mechanism by starting from a concrete
observation and tracing it down to first causes, marking what is settled and what
is open (the kind of explainer the field rates for making a mechanism click). The
craft problem is keeping the causal chain honest and concrete and holding a
register that neither overstates ("models ignore the middle") nor shrugs. Do not
let an exemplar supply this article's own findings.
