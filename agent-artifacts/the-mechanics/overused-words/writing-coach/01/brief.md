This lesson explains why AI writing overuses certain words, working from an
everyday behavior down to the training mechanism, marking what is settled and what
is guesswork. Study writers who explain a system's behavior in plain language and
are precise about the limits of what is known: good mechanistic explainer writing
and writing about language and corpora. The subject invites cleverness about
word-choice; pick exemplars who are witty only when the nouns carry it, since the
piece must not become a showcase of the words it describes. Do not quote passages
that hand the writer the mechanism or the specific-word hypothesis.
