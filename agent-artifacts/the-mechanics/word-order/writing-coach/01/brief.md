# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/word-order (01)

Inputs:
- `../../commission.md` — the assignment: from the behavior (the model respects
  word order) down to the mechanism that supplies order, since attention will not.
- `../../editorial-direction.md` — house standard, the paper's voice, series prompt.

Output: `voice-guide.md` (this directory)

This round's focus: the piece explains a counterintuitive mechanism — that a
transformer's core operation is order-blind and order is handed to it — to a
smart reader with no math background beyond algebra and no code. Study writers
who explain a technical mechanism from a concrete everyday effect, who use a
small worked example to carry an abstract point, and who are honest about the
edge between settled engineering and open research without hedging into mush.
Prefer explainers of systems (not opinion or narrative writers) who stay plain.
