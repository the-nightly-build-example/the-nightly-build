## What this piece is
A lesson that works backward from a familiar behavior (a chatbot answering "7"
when asked for a random number) to the mechanism that produces it, and stops when
it hits ground. Domain: how language models generate text. Genre: explanatory
writing that traces one observed behavior down to its cause, step by step, with
small concrete examples and no code.

## What the voice guide is for
Study writers who explain a mechanism by starting from something the reader has
seen and taking it apart one honest step at a time, and who are comfortable
saying which parts are settled and which are open. The best exemplars here trace
cause without hand-waving and keep the small example in view. Find them among
writers the field rates for mechanism explanation.

An exemplar that itself explains LLM sampling or the "random number" behavior
needs care: quote it only where the passage shows the writing and not the
findings this article will report, or choose a different piece.
