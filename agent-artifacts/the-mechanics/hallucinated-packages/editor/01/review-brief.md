Round focus:
- Distinctness from the hallucination lesson is the load-bearing editorial question. The piece
  must own repeatability + the supply-chain risk and LINK the-mechanics/hallucination for the
  base mechanism, not re-teach "why models invent things." If it re-teaches, that is a
  required cut.
- Honesty of the threat state: the prose must say the threat is demonstrated-plausible, not
  realized in the wild (the one real upload was a harmless PoC), and must not lead with or
  overclaim the era-dependent "~20%" rate. Confirm the repeatability numbers (within-run
  recurrence, cross-generation persistence) match the evidence's Numbers section exactly.
- Settled-vs-open line: the recurrence is established empirically; the token-level "why this
  exact name recurs" is synthesis resting on the hallucination lesson. Confirm the prose marks
  that honestly and does not assert a proven token-level cause.
- Writer flagged: secondary source display titles (the Lasso, Register, and Help Net entries)
  were rendered from the evidence descriptions/URL slugs, not verbatim headlines. Verify each
  citation's href resolves to the source itself and that the printed publisher/title is
  accurate; fix a miscitation if the right source is at hand, or route it.

My recent-pattern notes (catch formulas no single article can show; compare against the recent library):
- Dek molds recurring across the paper: the two-clause "claim, and/so the twist"; the
  comma-triad (three clauses, comma-joined, closed with "and"); the "The [thing] that..."
  opener. Cut on sight if present.
- Closing body heading mold: recent mechanics/instruments/when-ai-breaks pieces end on a terse
  verdict-of-limits heading ("A self-report is no proof of who built it," "What a utilization
  number cannot see," "The same setup is answering support questions right now"). Keep the
  required where-it-lives / what-fixes-it content; revise the heading if it is built to that stamp.
- Avoid "The line the paper/authors drew itself" heading (used by chain-of-thought, sleeper-agents).
- Furniture reflex: nb-note as the default "voice" block, and nb-stat-strip/nb-table by habit.
  Remove any component doing no work; add one only where presentation genuinely helps.
- The orientation heading should be its own concrete step, not a paraphrase of the headline.
