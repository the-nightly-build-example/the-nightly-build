# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/text-in-images (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (at the artifact root): house standard, paper voice,
  lesson template rules, the-mechanics series prompt.

Output: agent-artifacts/the-mechanics/text-in-images/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

The article: a lesson that starts from a behavior everyone has seen — garbled
text in AI-generated images — and works backward, step by step, to the cause,
naming the real parts of the system in plain words. No code. Genre:
mechanism explainer for a serious general reader. Length band 1200-2200 words.

This round's focus: study writers who explain how a system produces a
surprising behavior by tracing cause backward one concrete step at a time,
using small vivid examples, without jargon walls and without hand-waving.
Exemplars need not be about image generation; if one is, quote only where the
passage shows the writing, not the mechanism.
