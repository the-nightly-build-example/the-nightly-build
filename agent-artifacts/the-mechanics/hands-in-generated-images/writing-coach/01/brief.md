failure (six-fingered hands) to the mechanism inside a diffusion model, keeping
each step concrete. Study writers who explain a system's inner workings from a
familiar surface down to a real cause, and who can make a technical failure vivid
without hand-waving or cheap jokes. Exemplars on image generation itself may be
quoted only where the passage shows the writing, not the mechanism.
