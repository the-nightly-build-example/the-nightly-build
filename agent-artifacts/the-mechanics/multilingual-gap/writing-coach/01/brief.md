# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/multilingual-gap (01)

Inputs:

- `commission.md` (at the artifact root) — the behavior, angle, neighbors, and
  habits not to inherit.
- `editorial-direction.md` (at the artifact root) — house standard, slop, headline
  standard, press voice, lesson identity, and the series prompt.

Output: `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md` (under this article's artifact root).

This round: the genre is a mechanism explainer that starts from a behavior anyone
who has used AI in a second language has noticed and works backward to its causes,
one real part at a time, for a smart reader new to how these systems work. Study
writers who make a system's internals legible: who take an everyday behavior apart
into real parts, use a concrete example to make each step land, and never skip a
step. Prefer explainers precise about a mechanism to ones who reach for analogy.

Break the paper-wide opener formula ("By the end you will know X. You will also
see Y") and do not model The Mechanics' recent "the thing that feels like X is not
what happens" opener.

Run environment: outbound web is available through a proxy. Retrieve the raw text
of every exemplar page and verify each quotation word for word against it before
reporting, per your skill.
