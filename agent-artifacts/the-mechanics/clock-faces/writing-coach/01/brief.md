The domain is a mechanism explainer: it starts from a quirky, concrete behavior
(an image generator drawing every clock at 10:10) and works down to the parts of
the system that produce it. Study writers who explain how a machine-learning
system actually works from a everyday observation, in plain language, keeping the
curiosity of the puzzle without cutesiness. The register is plain and concrete,
no hype and no doom. Do not draw voice from prior Nightly Build articles.
