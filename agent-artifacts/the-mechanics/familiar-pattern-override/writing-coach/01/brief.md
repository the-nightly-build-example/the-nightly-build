# writing-coach brief: the-mechanics/familiar-pattern-override (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment: why a lightly-changed famous riddle gets
  the canonical wrong answer, worked backward to the mechanism, settled vs. open.
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, and the series prompt.

Output: ./voice-guide.md

This is an explanatory "how does it actually do that" lesson for a smart,
widely-read reader with no time in a codebase, no code. Study writers who explain
a counter-intuitive machine behaviour from a familiar example, keep a clean line
between what is settled and what is disputed, and resist over-claiming. Choose
exemplars whose craft carries over to teaching a mechanism and its open questions;
do not pick pieces that supply this lesson's own explanation.
