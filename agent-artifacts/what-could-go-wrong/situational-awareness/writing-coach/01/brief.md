- `../../commission.md` — the assignment: state the situational-awareness risk
  argument at full strength, then draw the line between what has been measured

- `../../editorial-direction.md` — house standard, the paper's voice, series prompt.

Output: `voice-guide.md` (this directory)

This round's focus: the piece has to steelman a safety argument fairly, then test
it against evidence without tipping into either alarm or dismissal — the desk's
hardest tonal task. Study writers who can hold a contested technical claim at
arm's length, present the strongest version of a view they may not share, and
mark the exact edge of the evidence in plain language. Prefer writers on science
or risk who are scrupulous about "shown" versus "argued," and who never let a
vivid worry outrun its proof. Avoid anyone whose register is advocacy.
