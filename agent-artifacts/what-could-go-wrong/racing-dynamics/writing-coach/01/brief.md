## Domain and genre
Explaining a formal risk argument (a small game-theoretic model of AI
development competition) and then testing it against evidence, for a smart lay
reader. Genre: the steelman-then-scrutinize essay that presents an argument at
full strength before weighing it, and marks analogy apart from demonstration.
Study writers the field rates for reasoning carefully about risk and incentives

and game-theory explainers who keep their thumb off the scale). Pick your own

company as an authority.

## What the voice guide must solve for this article
- Presenting a model's logic honestly and generously, then auditing its
  assumptions, without the voice tipping into either doom or dismissal.
- Explaining an equilibrium and a counterintuitive result (more information →
  less safety) in plain words, so the reader follows the mechanism.
- Keeping reported fact, the model's if-then claims, and the writer's synthesis
  visibly distinct.
- Correcting overconfidence on either side through evidence, not through a
  "not X but Y" reflex.
