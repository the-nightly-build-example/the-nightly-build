## Cut

Cut: 2 sentences reworded, 1 clause cut, 1 clause narrowed (0 sentences
removed outright — nothing failed the delete test entirely, but four
passages needed tightening); worst tell: "The research behind this
lesson found..." — a summary of the piece's own research process
(near-paraphrase of the writer brief's "the researcher found no technical
rebuttal") standing in for a flat claim about the world.

Direct edits made:
1. **Self-reference in the curse-of-information section.** "The research
   behind this lesson found plenty of people rejecting... It found no
   technical critique..." described the research process rather than
   stating the epistemic gap. Reworded to "Plenty of people reject the
   racing framing's real-world premise, covered below. But no technical
   critique addresses whether this ranking survives..." — same content,
   no self-grading, no new claim.
2. **Unsupported inferential clause in the testing-time section.** "too
   short for the testing it normally runs" was an editorial gloss beyond
   METR's quoted text, and redundant with the next sentence's quote
   ("more thorough evaluations are likely to reveal additional
   capabilities"), which already carries the same implication on the
   record. Cut.
3. **Report-vs-claim slip in the takeaway.** "The safety-speed tradeoff
   is real too" restated a partly-supported claim (per the brief:
   "partly supported... a primary admission of the incentive, not proof
   the field cuts safety") as settled fact, in the piece's most visible
   position, contradicting the properly hedged framing two sections
   earlier ("That is one developer's account, not proof the field bends
   to it"). Changed to "has a genuine admission behind it too," echoing
   the body's own hedge language rather than overclaiming.
4. **Scope overreach on Scharre.** "a pace nothing in AI competition has
   matched" broadened Scharre's claim (which is explicitly about
   *military* AI spending, per the evidence record's scope caveat: "this
   piece is about military AI competition between states, not the
   commercial frontier-lab competition the commission centers") to cover
   AI competition generally, including the commercial race the rest of
   the piece is about. Narrowed to "a pace military AI spending has not
   matched."

Checked and left alone: the "Why this matters" bookend's closing
signpost sentence ("This lesson builds the model... then checks its
