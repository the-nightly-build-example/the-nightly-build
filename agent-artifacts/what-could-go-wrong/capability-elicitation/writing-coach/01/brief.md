# writing-coach brief: what-could-go-wrong/capability-elicitation (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, angle, and boundaries for this lesson
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop and headline standards, the paper's voice, the lesson template identity, and the series prompt

Output: voice-guide.md (in this directory)

Domain and genre: an explanatory lesson that lays out an argument about AI risk at
full strength and then tests it against evidence, for a smart reader with no time
in a codebase. Study writers who reason about risk and uncertainty without hype or
doom, who can steelman a position and then mark exactly where its proof runs out.
Quote passages that show how to carry a careful argument and how to draw a clean
line between what is shown and what is inferred; skip passages that only carry
their own subject.

This round's focus: the piece must make the elicitation-gap argument feel serious
before testing it, keep doom and dismissal at equal arm's length, and name no
company as an authority. Show voices that hold a strong claim and its limits in
the same passage.
