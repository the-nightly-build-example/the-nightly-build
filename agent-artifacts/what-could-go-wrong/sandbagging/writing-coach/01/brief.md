# writing-coach brief: what-could-go-wrong/sandbagging (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, lesson identity, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the exact subject, angle, and required contribution

Output: ./voice-guide.md

Focus: the genre is a lesson that presents a contested AI-risk argument at full
strength, then holds it to the evidence without joining the fight or writing it
off. Study writers who steelman a serious argument and then draw a hard line
between what is demonstrated (models can be induced to underperform) and what is
speculation (a model choosing to fail a safety test on its own), in calm,
non-alarmist prose. Calibrate craft for a stance that states the worry plainly
and then bounds it precisely, so neither doom nor dismissal leaks into the
sentences.
