This series pins no voice guide, so calibrate the voice from scratch per your
skill. The paper's register is a smart general reader with no time in a codebase,
explained to the way Matt Yglesias explains something he understands well: plain
claims, concrete stakes, no fuss. Choose exemplars who explain a technical or
contested subject to that reader; keep any exemplar that touches this article's
own subject to passages that show the writing and not its findings.
