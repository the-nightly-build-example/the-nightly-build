## Original-work sentence
The article threads eight separate primary documents into one teachable line
that none of them draws on its own: that the strongest proponents of AI moral
concern and its sharpest opponent agree on what today's systems have actually
been shown to do, and that the whole live disagreement is only over which error
— over-attribution or under-attribution — is the worse one to make. The work is
visible in the "What a working system has actually been shown to do" and "Which
error would be worse" sections, where the proponents' own admissions (weak
self-reports, the gaming problem) are set against Suleyman's confident negative
to locate the real fault line.
