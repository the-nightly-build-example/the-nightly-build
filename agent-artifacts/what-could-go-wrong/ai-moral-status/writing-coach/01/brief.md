## What this piece is
A lesson that presents a contested argument at full strength, then tests it
against evidence and draws a line between what is demonstrated and what is
analogy. Subject: the moral status of AI systems / model welfare. Domain:
philosophy and the science of mind as the AI field reasons about them. Genre:
even-handed argumentative explanation that steelmans both sides and leaves the
verdict to the reader.

## What the voice guide is for
Study writers who can hold a hard, emotionally loaded argument at arm's length:
who present a position at its strongest, test it honestly, and refuse both hype
and dismissal. The best exemplars here reason about contested questions (in
philosophy, science, or policy) without tipping into advocacy or sneer, and keep
the reader oriented to what is known versus assumed. Find them among writers the
field rates for careful argument.

An exemplar that itself argues about AI consciousness or moral status needs
care: quote it only where the passage shows the writing and not the position this
article will weigh, or choose a different piece. The register is even-handed and
serious; do not pick passages whose force comes from taking a side.
