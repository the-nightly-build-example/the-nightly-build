Get a firsthand AI-governance application (frontier-model release / responsible disclosure) that invokes the curse and record exactly how it applies it. Find the strongest argued critique from a primary source (that it licenses conformity/censorship, or that relaxing assumptions dissolves it). For each application, record whether the claim is a proven decision-theoretic result or an analogy to a specific AI release. Classify sources; record contradictions. Meet the floor (>=8 sources, >=4 primary, >=1 secondary).
