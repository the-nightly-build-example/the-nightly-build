# writing-coach brief: what-could-go-wrong/mind-crime (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, paper voice, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, the desk's stance, and its boundaries

Output: voice-guide.md (this directory)

This round's focus: an explanatory lesson that lays out a speculative, contested
argument at full strength, tests it, and leaves the reader to judge, without joining
or dismissing it. Study writers who present a hard or unsettling idea fairly and
precisely to a general reader while holding their own verdict in check. Exemplars
need not be about AI. Avoid exemplars whose subject is AI consciousness, digital
minds, or existential risk, so no framing or finding leaks into the guide.
