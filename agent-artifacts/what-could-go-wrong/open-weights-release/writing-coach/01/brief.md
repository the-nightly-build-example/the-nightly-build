Output: voice-guide.md (this directory)

The article is a lesson that teaches a contested AI-risk argument (open model-
weight release as irreversible proliferation risk) fairly, steelmanning both
sides and marking where evidence runs out, for a smart non-technical reader.
Study writers who reason about risk and policy under uncertainty with fairness
and restraint — who can hold an argument at full strength before testing it, and
who separate what is shown from what is feared, without joining a side or writing
one off. That even-handed, evidence-first register is the craft problem here; the
paper voice forbids both hype and doom.
