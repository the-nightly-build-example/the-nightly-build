Subject: a lesson teaching the takeoff-speeds argument, fast and discontinuous
versus slow and continuous AI progress. It steelmans the fast case, tests it
against what real systems have done, and draws a clean line between what is shown
and what is analogy. For a smart non-practitioner.

The genre is careful argument writing: present a contested claim at full strength,
then weigh it against evidence without joining a side or writing it off. Study
writers who explain a genuine disagreement fairly, who can state the strongest
version of a view they may not hold, and who keep alarm and dismissal at the same
distance. Choose exemplars for how they sound. If an exemplar covers AI takeoff,
existential risk, or these specific authors, quote only passages that show the
writing, not their positions, so the writer inherits craft and not conclusions.
