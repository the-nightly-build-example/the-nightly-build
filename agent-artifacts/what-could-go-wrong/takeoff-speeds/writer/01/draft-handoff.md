## Original work

This article makes METR's single measured time-horizon line the physical hinge
of the whole debate, showing the one six-year curve is at once the continuity
camp's proof of a smooth climb and the fast camp's runway, so the finding it
lands is not which side is right but that the only thing anyone has measured
cannot adjudicate between them, and that continuity is not the reassuring side.

The evidence record notes in passing that the METR trend "cuts both ways"; the
piece turns that note into the article's spine: it stages the trend as one
object (the captured Fig. 1), draws the shown-versus-projected line in takeoff's
own particulars against it, and resolves the disagreement not as fact-vs-fact
but as a dispute over which chart even counts (the comparison table).
