Focus: This lesson steelmans a scary AI-safety argument and then holds it to the
evaluation record without joining the alarm or writing it off. Study writers who
argue about high-stakes risk with calm, evidence-first discipline — who can make
a serious case at full strength and then draw a clean line between what has been
shown and what is conjecture (the best AI-safety and risk-analysis writers, and
rigorous science writers on contested threats). The craft problem: keep the
temperature down while the subject invites drama; make "how much did the harness
hand the model" a precise, load-bearing distinction; earn any grave word.

Recently used, do not reuse: this series' "the model that X needs Y nobody has
held" comma-construction dek and the "an optimizer wrote / an agent with a
correct reward" agent-noun opener. Advise moves that let the evidence set the
tone.
