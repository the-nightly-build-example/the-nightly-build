and the self-replication paper at their methods and results, not summaries.

Questions the evidence must answer:
1. Where ARA (autonomous replication and adaptation) was defined as a
   dangerous-capability threshold and by whom: the exact framing in evaluation
   bodies' and labs' frameworks (METR; UK AISI; OpenAI Preparedness Framework;
   Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy; Google DeepMind Frontier Safety
   Framework). Quote the specific capability each names and the threshold logic.
2. What real ARA/autonomy evaluations have measured: the concrete task suites
   (e.g. METR's autonomy tasks — acquiring resources, copying a model to a new
   server, setting up an inference API) and the reported results — which
   sub-tasks named models complete unaided, which they fail, and how much
   scaffolding/hints the harness supplies. Get exact pass rates or task-count
   results with the model and date.
3. The Fudan 2024 paper claiming frontier LLMs "can self-replicate": exact
   claim, models tested, the setup (what the researchers pre-built, what hints
   and agent scaffolding were provided, what "successful replication" counted
   as). State precisely how hand-held the result was. Find at least one
   independent, careful critique or a contrasting eval.
4. The present-day argument: who invokes ARA now and what policy they attach
   (if-then commitments, pre-deployment evals, capability thresholds) — from
   the primary framework documents and recent eval updates. The most recent
   result on how close frontier models are to the threshold.
5. Contradictions to surface: alarmist framings that read a scaffolded task
   success as autonomous replication, vs dismissive framings that read "no wild
   replication" as impossibility. Record both with their primaries.

In Numbers, record every pass rate / task result with owner, the exact model and
version, the scaffolding conditions, and the date. Flag clearly any figure whose
meaning depends on how much the harness supplied. Contradictions section must be
filled after a real search for the strongest version of each side.
