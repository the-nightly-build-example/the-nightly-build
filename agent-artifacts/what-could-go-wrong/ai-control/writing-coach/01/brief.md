This lesson presents a current AI-safety agenda at full strength, then holds it to
what the experiments have actually shown versus what is still projection. Study
writers who explain an adversarial or security-style argument fairly and then
test it, keeping demonstrated results separate from extrapolation, without hype
or dismissal. Value clear reasoning about threat models and honest treatment of
what a toy experiment does and does not prove. Choose passages that show
fair-minded evaluation of a proposed safeguard and clean separation of evidence
from projection, not passages that carry claims about AI control, scheming, or
alignment themselves.
