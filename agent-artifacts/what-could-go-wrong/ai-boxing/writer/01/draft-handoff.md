## What the article does to the evidence

It sets the persuasion strand and the machine-escape strand side by side and holds
both to a single test the evidence record never applies across them: the article
shows that the AI-box "wins" (humans role-playing the AI in unlogged, anecdotal
games the gatekeeper usually wins) and the model "self-exfiltrations" (real steps
taken only inside sandboxes where researchers supplied the goal, the knowledge, and
the exit) both fail the same measure — nothing has broken out of a box unaided — so
the live disagreement is neither doom nor dismissal but the untested extrapolation
of today's monitoring to systems far more capable than any yet built. The evidence
record documents the two strands separately; the article unifies them under that one
test and locates the remaining gap.
