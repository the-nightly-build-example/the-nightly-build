# researcher brief: what-could-go-wrong/companion-dependency (01)

Inputs:
- ../../commission.md — the assignment, four-beat structure, boundaries, and the
  "name no company as an authority" constraint.
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader.

Output: ./evidence.md

Sourcing floor (nb source-policy): at least 8 sources, at least 4 primary, at
least 1 secondary. Work from original documents.

Primary documents to read and record with locators:
- Sherry Turkle, "Alone Together" (2011) and/or her primary papers. Verify the
  argument's origin: what she observed (studies of people with sociable robots and
  screens) and her claim that we would come to prefer frictionless simulated
  connection. Get her actual wording where the framing matters.
- The recent companion-use wellbeing studies, read firsthand: the 2025 MIT Media
  Lab / OpenAI work on affective use of ChatGPT (the randomized study and the
  large-scale platform analysis) — verify the actual findings, including the
  heavier-use / loneliness associations and any short-term benefits, the sample,
  and the design. Record what is correlational vs. randomized.
- At least one independent (non-company) study of companion-app users (e.g. Replika
  user studies, or a peer-reviewed study on chatbot companionship and loneliness).
  Verify effect direction and size, and note self-selection.
- The primary record of the Replika episode where a product change (removal of
  erotic roleplay / "ERP", early 2023) distressed users who had formed bonds —
  from the primary record (the company's own communications, the community record,
  and any regulator action such as the Italian Garante order). Verify dates and
  what actually changed.

For "who makes the argument now and what they want done": record named present-day
proponents and their proposed responses (age limits, anti-manipulation design
rules, disclosure, crisis handling), from their own writing/testimony where possible.

Company-run studies: record the producer and its stake explicitly; treat as one
dataset, never as an authority. Flag any finding only a company source supports.

Contradictions (search hard): the strongest evidence that companions cause
dependence and displace human connection, AND the strongest evidence that they
help some lonely users or that the harm claim outruns the longitudinal data.
Record both in full, with study limits.

Numbers section: each cited effect (loneliness/wellbeing association, usage
figures, the Replika episode's scale) with its owning primary, sample, and period.

Source assets: note any figure/table that could carry the argument. Do not
prescribe crops.

Do not browse the repository for background. Confirm every URL resolves. Flag any
commission claim the evidence cannot support.
