This lesson lays out a contested argument about AI companions and human
relationships fairly, then tests it against psychological and usage evidence, for
a smart, widely-read reader with no time in a codebase. Study writers who handle
technology-and-society arguments and human wellbeing without moralising or
sentimentality, who steelman before they weigh, and who keep social-science
findings honest about their limits. Choose exemplars whose craft carries over to
fair treatment of this argument; do not pick pieces that supply its conclusion.
