The domain is an argument piece: it states a risk argument at full strength, then
tests it against evidence, judging it on the merits without joining the alarm or
the dismissal. Study writers who lay out a contested argument fairly and then
weigh it against evidence — human-factors, safety-engineering, or careful
science writers who can steelman a case and then say where it holds. The register
is serious and even-handed, no hype and no doom, a grand word only after the
argument earns it. Do not draw voice from prior Nightly Build articles.
