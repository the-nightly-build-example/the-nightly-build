  problem: capabilities generalization, and the sharp left turn' (and any careful
  restatement). Record the claim in the author's own terms: capabilities
  generalize far and fast while trained-in alignment does not, so safety can
  break at a broad capability jump. Record the stated reasoning and what
  evidence, if any, the author points to.

  how narrow these are and what they do and do not show about a sudden global
  turn. Add any study measuring a safety property degrading as capability rises.
- The shown/analogy line: which parts have any working-system evidence and which
  remain reasoning about systems that do not exist.
- The present: who presses the argument now and what they want done; steelman the
  skeptics from their own writing (that capability and alignment are not cleanly
  separable; that scaling shows continuity, not a sharp turn).

observed, and that the goal-misgeneralization results are narrow. Record it.
