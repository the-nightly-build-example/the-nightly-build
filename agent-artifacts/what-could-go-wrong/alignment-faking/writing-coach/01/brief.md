The article lays out a contested empirical AI-safety result at full strength, then
tests it against what the experiment actually set up, for a smart reader new to it.
Study writers who explain a safety or scientific result fairly and then weigh it
soberly, without joining the alarm or dismissing it (the kind of writer the field
rates for reasoning about risk carefully). The craft problem is steelmanning a
striking result while being exact about how much the researchers arranged, and
drawing a clean demonstrated-vs-extrapolated line without the prose tipping into
alarm or snark. Do not let an exemplar supply this article's own findings or
verdicts.
