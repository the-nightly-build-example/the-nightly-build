Genre and domain: an explanatory lesson that weighs an argument about AI risk (that
AI will cause large-scale technological unemployment) for a smart, widely read
reader, steelmanning the case and the counter-case and separating measured evidence
from projection. The voice is even-handed, evidence-first analytical prose that
states a strong argument fairly, then tests it against numbers with their
denominators shown, and never tips into doom or dismissal. Study exemplars who
argue carefully about economics or technology's labor effects for a serious general
audience and hold competing evidence in view; pick passages that show fair-minded
weighing, not passages carrying this piece's own conclusions.

Run environment: web tools work through a configured proxy. Read each exemplar in
full from its own page and verify every quotation word for word before reporting.
