## Original work

The evidence record hands over Carlsmith's six raw credences, three
separately-sourced and mutually inconsistent reviewer critiques, a
superforecaster comparison, and an independent empirical survey as
disconnected facts and quotes; this draft is the first place they are
assembled into one teachable argument that keeps three things straight the
raw record does not itself state as lessons: that Soares's objection (the
conjunctive method understates the risk by construction) and
Thorstad's/the superforecasters' objection (specific premises overstate it)
are answers to different questions, not one "reviewers disagree" verdict;
that the evidence for premise 4's mechanism (specification gaming) is
demonstrated while power-seeking itself and premise 5's disempowerment are
not, which is the has-been-shown/still-analogy line the premises table and
prose are built to show side by side; and that Carlsmith's >10% revision
being unaccompanied by any revised premise table, paired with Thorstad's own
downward revision of his already-low number, is used to teach a general
point about what a moved probability does and doesn't license, rather than
being read as either an admission or a vindication in either direction.
