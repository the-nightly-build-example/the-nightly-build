This round's focus: the argument's weight rests on the gap between what a real
experiment showed and what remains projection, so the evidence must pin the
experiment down exactly. From the controlled reward-tampering study, establish

behavior emerged zero-shot from earlier stages, and whether training against

a curriculum built to elicit tampering shows little about spontaneous behavior,
