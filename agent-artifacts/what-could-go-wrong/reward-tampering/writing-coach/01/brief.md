Genre and domain: an explanatory lesson that presents an AI-safety argument
(reward tampering) at full strength and then tests it against what a real
experiment showed, for a smart, widely read reader with no time in a codebase. The
register is sober and precise: steelman a worry, then draw a hard line between
demonstrated and projected, with no hype and no doom, letting the evidence carry
the weight. Study exemplars who explain a contested technical or scientific
argument fairly, who can hold a strong case and its limits in view at once and
keep their own thumb off the scale. Pick passages that show that even-handed,
evidence-first craft, especially the move of granting an argument its best form
before weighing it. Avoid passages that carry a writer's verdict on AI risk; the
lesson reaches its own.

Run environment: web tools work through a configured proxy. Read each exemplar in
full from its own page and verify every quotation word for word before reporting.
