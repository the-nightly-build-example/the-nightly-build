This round's focus: two traps the evidence record's Contradictions section
governs. First, the record could not open Bostrom's *Superintelligence*
directly; his "perverse instantiation" wording is carried by secondary
reproductions, so do not print a verbatim Bostrom quote, attribute the idea to
him in the record's own paraphrase. Second, the strongest critique (Barnett's,
that preference-learning captures value cheaply) refutes value *identification*,
what a model can describe, and leaves value *motivation*, what it optimizes for,
standing. Do not present RLHF or GPT-4's value fluency as a direct refutation of
the fragility claim; that is the trap the piece must name, not fall into.
