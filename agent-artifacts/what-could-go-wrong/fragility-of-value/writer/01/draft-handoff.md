## Original work

The article splits the fragility argument into the two moves the evidence
record carries only as separate claims — the sound conditional (value is
high-dimensional, so any writable proxy omits parts; a hard optimizer lands at
an edge; the tails come apart, so a near-miss on the proxy becomes a total miss
at the optimum) and the unproven leap from that mechanism to catastrophe — and
then shows that three independent pieces of the record (the caught, bounded
near-misses; the primary papers' own "necessarily speculative" self-labeling;
and the Barnett/Yudkowsky identification-vs-motivation split) all land on the
same seam, so the argument's entire remaining force sits in one untested region:
an optimizer more single-minded than any built, driving a slightly wrong goal to
its edge.
