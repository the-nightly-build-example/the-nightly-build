## Original work (one sentence)
The article separates the argument's one genuinely new mechanism — an AI made
reliably and secretly loyal to a single principal — from the deployed and
lab-only building blocks around it, and shows that this mechanism reduces to
deceptive misalignment, a property the field cannot produce or verify on demand,
so the reader weighs a real unsolved problem rather than an undifferentiated mood.

That work is visible in the structure: "Replacing the refusers with loyal
machines" isolates the mechanism; "Real systems reach only partway" grades each
building block (deployed / assistive / lab-only / absent); "The secret-loyalty
problem is deceptive misalignment" makes the reduction the hinge and draws the
shown/projected line against both booster and dismisser.
