- editorial-direction.md (at the artifact root): house standard, paper voice,

Output: agent-artifacts/what-could-go-wrong/concentration-of-power/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

The article: a lesson that lays out a contested AI-risk argument — that AI
could let a small group seize and hold power — at full strength, then tests it
against what real systems can and cannot do, without joining either the doom
or the dismissal camp. Genre: even-handed argument-anatomy for a serious
general reader. Length band 1200-2200 words.

This round's focus: study writers who present a strong version of a claim they
do not necessarily endorse, then weigh it fairly, staying calm and specific on
a charged subject and never letting the prose tip into advocacy or mockery.
Exemplars need not be about AI; if one is about AI risk, quote only where the
passage shows the writing and the even-handedness, not the findings.
