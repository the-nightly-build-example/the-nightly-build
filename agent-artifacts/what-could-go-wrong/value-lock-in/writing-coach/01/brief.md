Output: ./voice-guide.md (beside this brief)

This lesson lays out a speculative AI-risk argument at full strength and then
tests it honestly, without joining the argument or writing it off. Study writers
who can present a contested idea fairly and then weigh it with a cool head:
analytic essayists and science writers who steelman before they judge and never
let rhetoric outrun the evidence. Pick exemplars whose register is calm and
precise, since the subject tempts grand language the paper forbids until earned.
Do not quote passages that hand the writer the argument's conclusions or the
counterarguments.
