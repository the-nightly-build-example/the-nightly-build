# draft-handoff: what-could-go-wrong/learned-deception (01)

## Original work (the one sentence)

This lesson turns the evidence record's condition column into a portable
three-question test that lets a reader place any "AI learned to deceive" claim
on one side or the other of the line between documented learned deception and
the still-unproven scheming claim, a distinction the sources draw in passing but
never operationalize for a reader.

Made visible on the page in the "How to tell them apart" note (the three
questions plus the verdict) and cashed out immediately on the clean case: the
"CICERO answers no three times" paragraph runs the test in front of the reader.
